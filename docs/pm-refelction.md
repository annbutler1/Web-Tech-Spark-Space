## How did you come up with your idea?
I wanted to build something genuinely useful for artists, so I thought about moments when I personally get stuck. That led me to the idea of a site that generates prompts, color palettes, and reference images to help break creative block. The concept came from combining my own experiences with what I felt digital tools could realistically support.

## How well did the collaboration with your developer work?
The collaboration worked well because we communicated early about what the vision looked like. I created detailed wireframes in Canva so the developer could see my exact layout, colors, and flow. That made it much easier to stay aligned and avoid misunderstandings about how the final site should look and function. I could hav ebeen more practive in checking the work for the final version but it was above and beyond what I expected. 

## What did you learn about writing requirements?
I learned that requirements need to be more specific than I expected. Instead of describing a feature conceptually, I had to explain what the user sees, what the button actually does, and what the output should look like. Clear requirements made development smoother and prevented unnecessary redesigns.

## Did you use any AI tools in this process? How?
Yes. I used AI to help brainstorm feature ideas, refine the descriptions of the tools on the site, and check whether my requirements were clear enough. It also helped me translate my vision into more technical wording. I learned AI is great for speeding up planning, but the actual creative direction still had to come from me.
